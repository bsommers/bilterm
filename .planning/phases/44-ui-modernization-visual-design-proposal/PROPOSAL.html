Bilterm visual proposal

The terminal stays the subject. Everything around it becomes one layered system: a quiet top bar, panes that share a material and a focus rule, drawers that grow out of the workspace, and floating tools in the same glass.

These are design mockups for sign-off, not renders of the app. The current app's renders are in .planning/phases/44-*/before/. Every profile below is the same layout driven by tokens only, which is the point of Phase 45.

Zen mode

One pane, no top bar, no status line. Controls come back on hover at the top edge, on Ctrl+X Z, or from the palette.

bill@host ~/src $

Design rules

  • Five materials: Background, Primary Glass (top bar), Secondary Glass (drawers), Floating Glass (palette, menus), Terminal Surface (always readable).
  • Focus is a brighter 1px edge plus a faint accent halo. No strong glow.
  • Pane titles show on the focused pane only. Actions appear on hover or from the palette.
  • In the text UI, glass is approximated with tint, bevel and rounded ╭╮╰╯ corners. In --gui, corners are vector arcs and the edge highlight is drawn in pixels. No true backdrop blur is claimed.
  • Master glass toggle falls back to opaque surfaces with the same layout.

Problems seen in the current renders

  • The built-in bitmap font draws all text in capitals, which reads as retro, not modern.
  • The open drawer shows a stair-step artifact at its lower left and its blurred rows stay noisy.
  • Headers repeat information: pane number, title, mode and hints all at once.
  • The prompt box takes three rows even when empty.

Decisions needed from you

  • Approve the direction (top bar with workspace pills, focused-pane title only).
  • Pick the default profile: Obsidian or Liquid.
  • Mixed-case GUI font: add a bundled proportional-free monospace, or keep the bitmap font as the Classic option only?
  • Should Zen mode hide the prompt box too, or keep it as a one-line input?
