## Contract deltas (the repo wins; downstream plans read these)

1. **`RowData` derives `Eq`** (contract section 3). Plan text said "RowData and FindRow must not derive Eq"; the two disagreed, and a superset derive breaks nothing. `FindRow` derives only `Clone, Debug, PartialEq`.
2. **`DiskCache` API** (plan 11): fields are private; use `is_loaded(kind)`, `cached(kind) -> &[FindRow]`, `store(kind, rows)`, `add_masked(n)`, `masked()`, `invalidate()`. `registry::saved` needs no edit to `types.rs`.
3. **`CMD_CAP` and `PANE_OUTBOX_CAP` are defined in `src/pane/capture.rs`** and re-exported by `registry::types` (the layer DAG forbids `src/pane` importing `crate::wm`). Values unchanged (512, 64); `caps_match_contract` asserts all twelve.
4. **`Ranked`** lives in `registry/rank.rs` (plan 04 owns it), re-exported as `registry::Ranked`. `RegistryState` also carries `cached_masked: [usize; 3]` next to `cached`.
5. **`FindOp::Close` also calls `invalidate_find_cache()`.** `Open` calls `run_find` (which clears the cache first) and stores results with `set_find_results`.
6. **`:finder query` with no text is `ClearQuery`**; with text it is `SetQuery(text)`. `:finder kind` accepts the plural words too.
7. **Status message**: `n` in `<n> results` is `total` (rows may be truncated); the live/saved/log breakdown counts returned rows. Matches contract text when not truncated.
8. **Key-table test**: the plan's literal "no modal row equals a leader row" cannot hold (`Up Down PageUp PageDown Tab BackTab` exist in both). Leader rows are post-prefix chords and the modal swallows the prefix while open, so the test tolerates overlap on exactly those plain navigation keys and fails on any other. Global and pane tables are strictly disjoint.
9. `Action::sample` in the plan text is the `colon_expr` match inside `src/action.rs` tests; every `Find` variant maps to `":find agent"` there. `:finder` is NOT in `COLON_COMMANDS` (completion list), so the reachability test cannot use `:finder` as its colon expression; each `FindOp` colon form is covered by `finder_grammar_round_trips` instead.
